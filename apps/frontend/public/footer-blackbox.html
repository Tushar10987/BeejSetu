About Us

BeejSetu empowers farmers, FPOs, processors and buyers to capture full value from oilseed by-products. We combine predictive analytics, IoT-enabled quality verification, blockchain-based traceability and logistics optimization to connect smallholders with processors, traders and export opportunities — increasing farmer income while reducing post-harvest loss.
